🌌Exploring the Aurora

An aurora is a natural light display that appears in the night sky, usually near the Earth's polar regions. It happens when charged particles from the Sun interact with gases in Earth's atmosphere, creating colourful waves of light.

  • Aurora Borealis (Northern Lights)
  • Aurora Australis (Southern Lights)
  • Colours can include green, pink, purple, red, and blue.
  • They are most commonly seen in places such as Norway, Iceland, Canada, Alaska, and Antarctica.
