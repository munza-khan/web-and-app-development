Steve Jobs (1955-2011)

The Visionary Behind Apple

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, inventor, and visionary who played a major role in transforming the
technology industry. He was the co-founder of Apple Inc. and helped create products that changed the way people
use computers, phones, music, and digital technology.

From the Macintosh to the iPhone, Steve Jobs focused on combining technology with simple and innovative design.
