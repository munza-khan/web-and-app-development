About Steve Jobs

-Early Life

Steve Jobs was born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco, California. He was adopted by Paul and Clara Jobs, who raised him in Mountain View, California, in the heart of Silicon Valley. From a young age, Jobs was interested in electronics and technology. His father taught him how to work with tools and build things, which helped develop his curiosity and interest in engineering. Jobs attended Homestead High School, where he became interested in electronics and met people who shared his passion for technology. His early experiences in Silicon Valley played an important role in shaping his future career in technology.

-Education

Steve Jobs attended Homestead High School in California, where he developed an interest in electronics and technology. After graduating from high school in 1972, he enrolled at Reed College in Oregon. However, he dropped out after about one semester because he felt that traditional college was not right for him. Even after leaving college, he continued attending classes that interested him, including a calligraphy course. His experience with calligraphy later influenced his ideas about typography and the design of Apple products.

-Meeting Steve Wozniak

Steve Jobs met Steve Wozniak while he was still young, and they quickly became friends because of their shared interest in electronics and technology. Wozniak was skilled at designing and building electronic devices, while Jobs had a strong interest in ideas, design, and business. Together, they worked on different electronic projects and eventually developed the idea of creating personal computers. Their friendship and shared interests later led them to co-found Apple Computer in 1976, beginning what would become one of the most influential technology companies in the world.
